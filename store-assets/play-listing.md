internet permission, because it is the one a reader can verify on the listing page itself —

## Full description (4000 characters max)

```
No internet permission. Checkable on this page.

Scroll down to "App permissions": Deja is not allowed to use the internet. Android
enforces that, so your screenshots — bank balances, chats, tickets, IDs, one-time
codes — and the text read out of them cannot leave your phone. Not a promise in a
policy: a permission the app was never given.

FIND IT BY WHAT IT SAYS

You took the screenshot so you would have it later. Deja reads every one on your
phone so "later" is a search, not ten minutes of scrolling.

• Search in words: "swiggy receipt", "pnr", "wifi password"
• Search by time: "receipt last week", "tickets in june", "otp yesterday"
• Quoted "exact phrases" and -exclusions
• The matching words are highlighted in every result
• Reads English and Hindi (Devanagari — also Marathi, Nepali, Sanskrit), including
  mixed Hindi-English screens
• Decodes QR codes and boarding-pass barcodes: a Wi-Fi QR gives you the password, a
  UPI QR gives you the payee and amount

DOES SOMETHING WITH WHAT IT FINDS

A gallery shows you the screenshot with the number in it. Deja dials it.

• Call, message or save a phone number
• Copy a one-time code, a booking reference, a UPI ID, an IFSC
• Open a link
• Find every other screenshot that mentions the same booking or account
• Card, Aadhaar, PAN and passport numbers stay masked until you tap them

SORTED FOR YOU

Payments & bills, tickets & travel, codes & passwords, IDs, conversations, shopping,
places, food, work and more — and by the app each screenshot came from.

TELLS YOU BEFORE YOU LEAVE THE APP (optional)

Switch it on and Deja reads a screenshot the moment you take it. Screenshot an OTP
and a notification says what it found, with a Copy button — read on your phone, like
everything else. Off until you turn it on.

CLEAN UP WITHOUT FEAR

Duplicates, near-identical captures, old one-time codes, screenshots with nothing in
them — grouped, sized, and expandable so you choose individual screenshots rather
than all-or-nothing. Star the ones that matter and they are never offered for
removal. Removed screenshots go to your phone's trash, so they can still be restored.

FROM ANYWHERE

• Share any image into Deja and it becomes searchable too
• Home-screen widgets: your kept screenshots, and the latest code with Copy
• A Quick Settings tile that opens search in one swipe

YOURS

• No account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting
• Export everything Deja knows to a file you choose
• One control deletes the index and your search history
• Light and dark themes; English and Hindi

WHAT IT DOESN'T DO

Deja finds text. It does not understand pictures — "the one with the dog" needs a
cloud photo service, and Deja will never be one.
```
